This dashboard answers the following questions:

- How is my organization structured into organizational units and accounts?
- Which policy types are enabled at the root?
- Which service control, resource control, declarative and management policies are attached to each root, organizational unit and account?

The relationships graph hides accounts and AWS managed policies by default, and can be limited to one policy type. Policies attached only to accounts are shown when accounts are shown.

This dashboard must be run against the organization's management account or a delegated administrator account.
