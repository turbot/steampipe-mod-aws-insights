This dashboard answers the following questions:

- How many accounts and organizational units are in my organization?
- How many service control and resource control policies do I have?
- Which policies aren't attached to any root, organizational unit or account?
- Which member accounts have a custom service control policy in effect?
- Which roots, organizational units and accounts have the most service control policies attached?
- When did accounts join the organization, and how?

This dashboard must be run against the organization's management account or a delegated administrator account.
